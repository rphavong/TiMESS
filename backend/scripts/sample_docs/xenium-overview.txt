Xenium is a high-plex, single-cell resolution in situ spatial
biology platform. Xenium v1 supports gene panels up to 480 genes
per run and is optimized for FFPE tissue sections. Xenium Prime
extends this to panels of up to 5,000
genes while retaining single-cell spatial resolution, making it
suited to discovery-stage studies where the target gene set isn't
narrowed down yet.

Visium HD provides single-cell scale spatial resolution across an
entire tissue section using a continuous grid of 2-micron capture
areas, rather than targeted probes -- useful for whole-transcriptome
profiling without pre-selecting a gene panel. Visium HD 3' extends
this workflow to 3' gene expression chemistry.

Flex and Universal are single-cell (dissociated, non-spatial)
platforms: Flex uses a fixed, probe-based panel well suited to FFPE
and challenging samples, while Universal (3') captures the whole
transcriptome from fresh or frozen dissociated cells.
